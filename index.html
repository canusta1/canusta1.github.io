Yanlış Bilinenler Uploader

A Turkish short-video channel that explains, with sources, why commonly believed facts are wrong. "Yanlış Bilinenler Uploader" is a personal tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads videos that the channel owner has prepared and approved, via the YouTube Data API, only to the owner's own YouTube channel. It serves no other users and collects no third-party data.
